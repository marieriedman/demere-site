Flipside

Cover-screen and home-screen widgets for Home Assistant, made for the Galaxy Z Flip.

Beta: Flipside is still being tested and isn't in any app store yet.

If you came here from your Home Assistant's sign-in page: this is the app asking to connect. Signing in gives Flipside access to your Home Assistant from your phone only — nothing passes through this website.
